About SparkCircuit

Engineering Next-Gen Hardware

SparkCircuit is an emerging Indian technology studio founded by Azad Kumar with a vision to build innovative, reliable, and affordable electronic solutions for homes, businesses, and industries.

We specialize in electronics, IoT, and smart technology — from concept and prototype to real-world products. Our work covers Bluetooth amplifiers, home theater systems, smart LED controllers, IoT devices, embedded systems, PCB design, and custom hardware development.

Every circuit we design, every product we build, and every solution we create reflects our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction.
