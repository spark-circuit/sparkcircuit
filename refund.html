Our Guarantee

At SparkCircuit, we stand behind the quality of every product we sell. If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, we will make it right — with a replacement or a full refund.

7-Day Replacement Guarantee on all damaged or defective products.

2

Return Window

You can request a return within 7 days from the date of delivery.

  • Return request must be raised within 7 days of delivery
  • Requests after 7 days will not be accepted
  • Photo/video proof of the defect is mandatory
3

Eligible Products

Products are eligible for return if they are:

  • Damaged during transit — packaging damage, broken components
  • Dead on Arrival (DOA) — not working out of the box
  • Wrong item shipped — different from what you ordered
  • Manufacturing defect — with proper testing proof
4

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return:

  • Products damaged due to misuse, mishandling, or soldering
  • Products with physical damage caused by the customer
  • Custom-made or customized products
  • Products without original packaging or accessories
  • Consumables (solder wire, tape, glue, etc.)

Soldering or modification of any product will void the return eligibility immediately.

5

Follow these simple steps to request a return:

2
Share Photo/Video Proof Send clear photos or a video showing the defect, damage, or issue.
3

5
Get Your Replacement / Refund Once we receive the product, we'll process your replacement or refund.
6

Refund Methods

Refunds are issued via the same method you used for payment:

  • Online Payment (Razorpay): Refund to original card/UPI/bank account
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund via UPI or bank transfer to your account
  • Store Credit: Optional instant credit (with 5% bonus) for future orders

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong/damaged product).

7

Refund Timeline

Once your return is approved and the product is received, refunds are processed within:

  • 5–7 business days for online payments
  • 3–5 business days for COD refunds (after bank details verification)
  • Instant for store credit

Bank processing times may vary depending on your bank's policies.

8

Damaged in Transit

If your package arrives damaged:

  • Record a video while opening the package (recommended)
  • Take clear photos of the damaged packaging and product
  • Report to us within 48 hours of delivery
  • We'll handle the courier claim and ship a replacement immediately

Please do not accept packages that appear visibly damaged from outside. Refuse delivery and contact us immediately.

9

Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order from the "My Orders" section anytime before it is shipped:

  • Pending / Processing: Instant cancellation, full refund
  • Confirmed: Cancellation allowed with full refund
  • Shipped / Delivered: Cannot be cancelled — use return process instead
